# The Terminal

A cursor blinks in the dark,
patient as a lamp in a window.
I type a word, press enter,
and the machine considers it

the way rain considers a roof—
without opinion, without hurry—
then answers in small green letters:
done.

And somewhere a file grows by a line,
quiet as snow settling on a branch,
while two tools—write and edit—
carry the poem between them
like a bucket in careful hands.
